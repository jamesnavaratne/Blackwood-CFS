# Blackwood CFS v2.9.4.4 Tablet Landscape Trial RC2

RC2 retains the RC1 tablet-landscape layout and fixes UBD positioning at the opening 100% view.

## UBD interaction correction

- One-finger touch drag now pans whenever the UBD stage has scrollable map content, including at 100%.
- Mouse drag now pans the UBD stage at 100% and at zoomed levels.
- The opening zoom remains 100%; the fix does not artificially enlarge the map.
- Normal wheel scrolling, pinch zoom, double-tap zoom, double-tap-and-slide zoom, zoom buttons and saved map position remain available.

## Preserved from RC1

- Tablet landscape rotation and layouts.
- Side-by-side Directions and UBD panes.
- Persistent taskbar and all three saved workspaces.
- Reverse modal closing order.
- Inventory, Directions and Hydrants data.
